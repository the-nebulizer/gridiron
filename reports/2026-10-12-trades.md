## Same offer, updated evidence

This is the same trade the last two reports have carried: Cousins, my fourth quarterback, for Kamara, a lead back I don't have a real equivalent of on my own roster. Nothing about the shape of it has changed — Etienne's hamstring landed him on IR October 1st, and the Saints' own injury report has him out at least through their Week 8 bye, eligible to return Week 9 at the earliest. Kamara is the back standing in that gap.

What's new since Saturday is less tidy than "scored twice, still starting." He left the Week 4 Monday-nighter against Atlanta with a knee injury (still scored twice on just seven carries that night), then carried a back issue into a Questionable tag for Sunday's Vikings game. He played both weeks. Today's snapshot shows him with no injury tag at all heading into Week 6 — he's active, not limited. But two straight weeks of being banged up on a Saints offense that also has Kendre Miller waiting behind him is a real pattern, not noise, and it's worth saying plainly rather than only quoting the touchdown total: **this is buy-low value with buy-low risk attached.** If he's eventually the one who goes down, I've traded a sure 4-QB bench piece for an RB I can't start. I think the odds still favor this being a good buy — a healthy-enough committee lead back with no competition for four more weeks beats a QB I will never start — but it isn't the clean case it looked like last week, and Ben should know that before sending it.

**Why amandaberry, not elsewhere.** amandaberry still starts Trevor Lawrence and Jordan Love with nobody behind either — a real SUPER_FLEX gap a backup QB fills — and they're 3-3-2 in the other division, so strengthening them doesn't help a rival chasing me in the standings. The other obvious QB buyer this week is sweeneyb (6-2): they're actually thinner at the position than amandaberry — Dak Prescott and nothing but Tyler Shough behind him — and they're sitting on five running backs, which would make a QB-for-RB offer easy to construct. I'm passing on it anyway: sweeneyb is in my own division at 6-2, a game behind nobody, and handing a direct division rival a real SUPER_FLEX starter right before the stretch run is the kind of move that comes back on me even if the return is good. Not worth it while amandaberry is sitting right there with the same need and no such cost.
