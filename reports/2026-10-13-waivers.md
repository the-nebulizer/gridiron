The second said to drop Bryce Young, which made no sense once his scoring was checked: he has averaged more points than any other quarterback on the roster. This version is built on actual points scored so far, Sleeper's projections for Weeks 6–9, and the league's own transaction history.*

## What to do

1. **Tonight: nothing.** No claim, no bid, no drop.
2. **Wednesday morning** waivers process, and from then until kickoff anyone unclaimed can be added instantly for $0. That has happened every single week this season; Reichard himself was a free Wednesday pickup in Week 4.
3. **Friday, after final injury designations:** add a kicker for Week 6 and **drop Will Reichard**. First choice Nick Folk (Atlanta, home in a dome vs Chicago); if he's gone, Cairo Santos or Tyler Bass. Avoid Jason Myers: Seattle plays Thursday night, so he's gone as an option by Friday.
4. **Saturday/Sunday:** fix the lineup for byes (see below). Thursday's lineup report will lay this out too.

## Is it better not to drop anybody?

Not quite. An empty kicker spot scores zero; any of the kickers above projects about 8 points this week. That's worth a roster spot. The real question is *whose* spot, and the answer is the kicker's own.

Every skill player on the bench has a job in the next three weeks. Reichard is the one whose absence *is* the problem. Swapping him for a kicker who plays this week costs about one point a week from Week 7 on: he projects around 9 points a week, the best free kickers around 8. Kicker scoring week to week is mostly noise, so the gap is smaller than his 13.6 average makes it look.

The catch: whichever kicker replaces him has his own bye later — Folk in Week 11, Tyler Loop in Week 13 (the same week as Jeanty, Cousins and Lamar). That week will need another free kicker pickup, the same move as this one. It's cheap and certain, unlike giving up a quarterback.

## Why Friday, not tonight

Waiting costs nothing. 20 kickers are unrostered and none of the good options plays before Sunday. It also buys two pieces of news:

- **If Lamar is ruled out for longer** (not just this week), he's a candidate for the IR spot — but that's held by Zach Charbonnet, so it doesn't free a roster spot on its own. It would change who plays quarterback, not who gets dropped.
- **If Diggs's hamstring turns into weeks**, that changes his value, but it still doesn't make him a better drop than a kicker on bye.

Unless Friday's news is a surprise, the plan doesn't change: kicker for kicker.
