## What changed today

Stefon Diggs and Lamar Jackson both moved from Out to Questionable. That removes any case for trading away quarterback or receiver depth this week — if anything it makes your bench deeper, not thinner.

## The hunt

Checked all 11 other rosters for a mismatch worth offering on. Nothing stood out:

- Your only real shortage is kicker and defense, and nobody trades a serviceable kicker or defense mid-season — that's a streaming question, answered weekly on waivers, not a trade one.
- You're carrying four quarterbacks against two superflex starting spots, which is real depth to sell if the right buyer shows up. But every team in the league short at quarterback (four rosters carry only two) is already starting both of theirs every week — there's no buyer motivated enough to overpay, and Lamar's ankle makes this the wrong week to test the market on him specifically.
- Nobody else in the league is sitting on a back or receiver that would clearly upgrade a spot you start. Your lineup is set from true surplus, not from a hole a trade needs to fill.

## Standing watch

The trade deadline is Week 11 — five weeks out. Nothing on the forward outlook shows a playoff-week hole that can't still be fixed on waivers between now and then, so there's no clock pressure yet. Monday's hunt will look again, and sooner if a QB-needy team's own quarterback goes down.
