## What to do

1. **Decline sweeneyb's offer** (Keenan Allen for Cousins). It projects worse in every window: −0.7 this week, −5.5 over the rest of the season, −5 in the playoffs. Allen would be a fourth receiver who doesn't start.
2. **Send annaf23 Cousins and Juwan Johnson for Brock Bowers**, as one best-and-final offer and not as an opening bid. Johnson costs you about 1.8 points over the rest of the season and nothing in the playoffs. With Bowers here he'd be a third tight end, and he frees a bench spot. Anna has to drop someone to take two players. A message you can paste is on the card.

## Why Bowers, and why it's safe

I ran every one-for-one, two-for-one and one-for-two trade against all 11 other rosters through the points model. Kicker trades were left out. Only one trade both projects better for you in every window and gives the other manager a real reason to say yes. This is it.

**Your side:** Bowers averages 15.7 a game against Kraft's 8.1, and he gets 9–10 targets a game. Cousins is surplus: Lamar is due back as soon as Sunday, and that gives you three starting quarterbacks for two spots.

| How Lamar's return goes | This week | Next 3 | Rest of season | Playoffs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Back this week | +5.3 | +16.4 | +46.1 | +14.0 |
| Back next week | +4.6 | +15.7 | +45.4 | +14.0 |
| Never back | +4.6 | +15.7 | +42.4 | +11.0 |

It doesn't depend on Lamar. Without Lamar, Goff and Young still cover both quarterback spots every week, and losing Cousins on his own costs only 2–3 points over the rest of the season.

**The real risk is Bowers' health.** He had his meniscus trimmed in September and missed Weeks 1–2. He was limited in practice last week with a hamstring/calf issue, but he played (10.3 points) and carries no injury tag. If he missed the next three weeks, the trade would still be +29 over the rest of the season and +14 in the playoffs, but −0.7 this week. If he never played again, you'd lose Cousins' backup value: −2.7 over the rest of the season and −2 in the playoffs. So the most you can lose is a few points, and the likely gain is about 45. That's why it clears your bar.

**Their side:** annaf23 is 4-8-2 and starting Deshaun Watson and Tanner McKee at quarterback, with C.J. Stroud on the bench. In this scoring Cousins starts for them every week, and they keep Sam LaPorta to replace Bowers at tight end. Their own projection improves: +3.3 over the rest of the season and +7.5 in the playoffs. Bowers' name value is the obstacle. He's young and a natural keeper, so they may say no. If they do, nothing is lost.

**Calendar:** every week through the playoffs still fields a full lineup. Bowers' bye is Week 13, the same week as Jeanty's. Kraft covers it, and Kraft's own Week 11 bye is covered by Bowers.

## Why Johnson and not Washington or Cleveland

You wanted one offer, so the question was what to add. By Anna's own numbers, no add-on is worth much to her: Johnson changes nothing and Washington adds 0.3. That makes the add-on a gesture, so it should be the one that costs you least. Johnson costs 1.8 points over the rest of the season, and Bowers and Kraft still cover each other's byes (Weeks 11 and 13). Washington would leave you with only two receivers and no cover if McMillan or Diggs missed a game. Cleveland would leave Denver as your only defense through its Week 10 bye.

## What I passed on

- **Lamar-based deals.** The model can't see a player on IR, so at first it treated Lamar as free to give away. With his return counted, every Lamar deal projected worse than the Cousins version. Keep Lamar.
- **Jeanty plus a defense for Kenneth Walker (ObiWanless).** It projects well, but Jeanty is your natural keeper.
- **Irving plus Cousins for Jaxon Smith-Njigba (GehrmannJake).** It projects well for you, but it costs them 22 points over the next three weeks. They won't take it.
- **Adding a defense to the Bowers offer.** annaf23 has no defense in their lineup, so Cleveland or Chicago would make the offer sweeter. But it would force them to drop someone, and it collides with the Denver pickup and your Week 10–11 defense byes. Keep it as a sweetener only if they counter.
