## The idea

**Send:** Kirk Cousins (QB, LV)
**Receive:** Hunter Henry (TE, NE)
**Partner:** sweeneyb (roster 3)

**My side of the need:** I'm carrying 4 rostered QBs (Lamar, Goff, Cousins, Bryce Young) against one SUPER_FLEX slot. Meanwhile I have exactly one TE on the roster (Tucker Kraft, currently Questionable) and zero backup. They're also carrying two legitimate TEs (George Kittle, currently Questionable, plus Hunter Henry), which is redundant — Henry doesn't start over Kittle on a healthy week.

**Why THEY plausibly say yes:** Cousins is a straightforward Dak-insurance policy at a position (superflex QB2) where this league's 6-pt-pass-TD scoring makes even a mediocre streamer worth real bench value — a clear upgrade on Shough. Henry is their TE2 and does nothing for them behind a healthy Kittle, so it's low-cost for them to move.

**Why this is worth it for me:** fixes my single thinnest bench spot (zero TE depth behind a Questionable Kraft) at the cost of a QB I have three of, without touching my WR or RB depth (both already tight — only 3 usable RBs with Charbonnet on PUP/IR).
