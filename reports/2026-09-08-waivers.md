## Devaughn Vele (WR NO) — add, drop Keenan Allen

What changed since last week, when this report passed on Vele as a dart throw: Saints rookie Jordyn Tyson is out roughly two months with a hamstring, and Vele steps into the starting boundary role alongside Chris Olave. Over his final four games with the Saints last season he averaged just under 10 half-PPR points a game — real WR3/4 production. New Orleans opens as an underdog at Detroit then home to Baltimore, which should mean pass-heavy scripts and volume early. He's unrostered here and the second-most-added player on Sleeper right now (+268,912).

Keenan Allen is the drop: the older, lower-ceiling half of your two bench receivers, and already flagged last week as the most cuttable spot.

## Darren Waller (TE CAR) — optional, drop Kirk Cousins

You have no backup at tight end and Kraft carries a Questionable tag into Week 1. Waller signed with Carolina in August and is clearly the Panthers' top receiving tight end when healthy; he scored six touchdowns in nine games last year before a season-ending groin injury. But he's TE22 and touchdown-dependent, with Bryce Young throwing him the ball — 49 touchdown passes in 44 career starts. This is injury insurance, not an upgrade. Only take it if you want the depth.

If you do, Cousins is the drop — though note the correction since: he covers your Week 6 bye but not Week 13, when he's on bye alongside Lamar.
