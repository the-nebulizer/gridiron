Jackson's Questionable tag doesn't say why, so I checked. If that trade processes before Jackson's own status firms up and he turns out to be further out than day-to-day, SUPER_FLEX would have no healthy option behind him. This is a trade-timing risk, not a lineup problem today — Cousins is still here and still starting — but it's worth weighing before that offer is accepted this week rather than after.
