A tougher matchup for a clear lead tight end still beats a nicer matchup for a part-time one — same reasoning that kept Kraft over Johnson last week, and it applies again now that Gesicki's in the mix too. Worth knowing for later: Kraft is on bye in Week 11, and Gesicki is the one of the two bench tight ends who'd be picked up by the matchup if his snap share keeps climbing — watch it week to week.
