## Why Vele

Vele went for 7 catches, 69 yards and a touchdown in Week 1 (19.9 fantasy points) as the clear No. 2 option in New Orleans's passing game, playing a team-high 82 snaps. With Jordyn Tyson (hamstring) out at least three more weeks, Vele is New Orleans's WR2 by role, not by injury-luck — he's a real weekly starter-caliber flex, and he's showing up on Week 2 "must-add" lists across the fantasy press. He's still unrostered in this league (nobody else has claimed him despite the highest add count on the board), but that won't last through Tuesday night. Bid aggressively: $15 of your untouched $100 FAAB is well within budget even after reserving for Week 13 (QB, ~$20) and Week 7 (K/DEF, ~$10) per the season plan.

## Second look: Michael Mayer (TE, LV) — smaller bid, needs a second drop

Mayer started for the Raiders in Week 1 with Brock Bowers out after a meniscus procedure, and he's still unrostered here. But Bowers is now listed questionable to return as soon as this coming week — the Raiders think there's a real chance he's back for the Chargers game — so Mayer's expanded role could be a one-week rental rather than a sustained job. Worth a speculative claim as your second move, but not at the price a longer runway would justify: **bid $6**, and only if you're willing to drop Jakobi Meyers (buried behind Diggs, McMillan, and now Vele on your bench; his snap share hasn't moved). That drop isn't available yet either way — Meyers is part of the Cousins offer out to Tally241, so this claim is the fallback to make only if that trade is declined by Tuesday night. Skip it entirely if you'd rather not thin the wide receiver bench further.
