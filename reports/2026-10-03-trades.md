Allen was already surplus depth sitting on my bench; the Out tag just means he won't show anything this week to make him look more valuable to a trade partner, nor less valuable as a stash — he's a proven starter when healthy, and a one-week groin issue isn't the kind of thing that tanks his value. Nothing to do here.

## Rescanned all 12 rosters: still no quarterback buyer for Kirk Cousins

Every other team in the league still starts two able-bodied quarterbacks. The same three teams flagged last check — sweeneyb, amandaberry, and sparkJ88 — are still the only ones running exactly two healthy quarterbacks with no backup; still nothing to offer while all six of those starters are healthy today.

But they already have Jameson Williams sitting on their bench as a ready replacement, so there's no urgency on their end and nothing to extract. Same read as the last check on their roster; nothing's changed there.
