> Refreshed Oct 3 after a roster change: Keenan Allen now Out

## Keenan Allen is Out — not a reason to move him

Allen's groin issue, which showed up limited on Thursday and non-participant Friday, has him officially Out this week. He's been bench depth all season, so this costs nothing in the lineup. The question worth asking out loud is whether it's worth dropping him now, since he'll score zero either way and both the bench and the one IR slot are completely full. The answer is no: wide receiver isn't short anywhere in the lineup — McMillan, Diggs, and Jakobi Meyers cover it fine — so this would be swapping one piece of unused depth for another, not fixing a need. A groin issue is typically a short-term, week-to-week thing rather than a long absence, and Allen is a proven NFL starter when healthy. There's no clearly superior free agent worth giving him up for on a one-week designation. Hold.

The IR slot, for the record, is still occupied by Zach Charbonnet (still on PUP, not yet eligible to return), so there's nowhere to put Allen even if it were worth doing — and it isn't.

## Kicker and defense: hold both, same read as last check

Will Reichard is at home against Miami; the Bears are at home against the Jets. Neither needs a look before Sunday.

## Good news: Bucky Irving's running-back risk is gone

Irving has since cleared his Questionable tag entirely and is a clean starter this week. That emergency plan isn't needed; no same-day pickup to make.
