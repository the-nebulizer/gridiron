**Claim Devaughn Vele (WR, NO) — no drop needed, bid $15.** He's still sitting unclaimed in this league a full day after topping the wire, and nothing about the picture has changed: 7 catches, 69 yards, a touchdown, and a team-high 82 snaps in Week 1, with Jordyn Tyson (hamstring) still out for at least three more weeks. Vele is New Orleans's real WR2 by role, not by injury-luck alone — a weekly flex-caliber start for as long as Tyson's out, and likely beyond if he keeps producing. He fills the one open bench spot outright.

## Second look: Mike Gesicki (TE, CIN) — the Week 11 answer, but blocked today

Kraft's Week 11 bye is the only week the outlook can't legally fill at any position, and Gesicki is the best fix on the board: he led the Bengals in catches, targets, and yards in Week 1 (5-78-1), running 81% of his routes from the slot as the clear starting tight end — not a committee piece and not contingent on someone else's injury. That's a real edge over Michael Mayer, whose Raiders snaps evaporate the moment Brock Bowers is cleared, and reporting since yesterday has Bowers targeting a Week 2 or Week 3 return — a short runway getting shorter. Passing on Mayer this week for that reason.

The problem is roster room. Every other bench body is spoken for: Cousins and Meyers are both in the trade offer out to Tally241 (Cousins + Meyers for Aaron Jones), and Bryce Young is in the separate offer to amandaberry — none of them can be dropped while those stand, and Sleeper shows no deadline on either answer. The one bench player who *isn't* tied up, Keenan Allen, looks like the obvious drop instead — except he isn't dead weight: with Diggs and Meyers both on bye Week 7, Allen is the only healthy WR left that week, and dropping him opens a *new* Week 7 hole at WR on top of the existing K/DEF one. Checked with the outlook tool rather than assumed.

So Gesicki rides as a fallback: claim him only if Tally241 declines the Cousins trade by Tuesday night, at which point Meyers frees up and becomes the drop — and swapping him for Gesicki costs nothing else (Meyers was already on Week 7's bye list; his bench spot buys the Week 11 TE fix with no new hole anywhere in the season). If the trade is accepted instead, Cousins and Meyers leave the roster on their own and this claim is moot — revisit the TE hole with the freed bench spot next week.

## Budget

$15 (Vele, certain) + $8 (Gesicki, contingent) leaves at least $77 of the $100 FAAB untouched. The season plan's reserves — ~$10 for the Week 7 kicker/defense bye pair and ~$10 for this same Week 11 TE hole if Gesicki doesn't land today, plus ~$15 held for a Week 13 QB upgrade — all still fit comfortably underneath that.
