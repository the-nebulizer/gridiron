## The claim: Fernando Mendoza, $6, drop Juwan Johnson

**Where he fits:** nowhere this week. He's QB4 behind Goff, Young and Cousins, and he projects 13.5 against Seattle. This claim isn't about this week's lineup. It's about three other things:

1. **Starting quarterbacks almost never reach waivers in superflex.** There are 32 NFL starters, and right now one of them is free. Every one of the other 31 is on a roster here. The rest of the free quarterbacks are backups.
2. **Who else wants him.** sparkJ88 is a direct rival. Both of their starters, J.J. McCarthy and Justin Herbert, are listed Questionable, Malik Willis is on IR, and they have $47 left. They've paid up to $53 for a player this season. amandaberry ($90) and Ranumova ($82, with Kyler Murray out) each start only two quarterbacks with no spare. The scoring model can't show this demand: Sleeper hasn't projected Mendoza past Week 10, so every team shows zero gain from him. That's a missing number, not a sign that nobody wants him.
3. **Cover and next year.** Cousins is changing teams in the middle of a week, and Lamar Jackson can't come back before Week 14. Mendoza is 23, and this is a keeper league, so a starting quarterback has value next season too. He is also a trade piece until the Week 11 deadline.

**The drop.** Juwan Johnson is my third tight end and has averaged 3.9 points over his last three games. Dropping him costs nothing this week and 1.6 projected points over the rest of the season. Mike Gesicki stays as Tucker Kraft's cover for Kraft's Week 11 bye, and Gesicki is also the better option of the two in this week's TE call. Dropping him opens no week on the calendar. Malik Washington and the Broncos defense cost about the same to drop, but cutting Washington would leave me only three receivers, and the Broncos are the defense that covers Houston later in the season.

**The price.** $6. He doesn't start for me, and the card's rule is that a player who doesn't start costs no more than a tenth of my budget. I'm keeping that rule rather than calling him a starter to get around it. $6 wins only if nobody else bids seriously. sparkJ88 ($47), amandaberry ($90) or Ranumova ($82) could beat it easily. If one of them does, I lose a player I wouldn't have started, and I keep $61 for injuries over the last six weeks. Kickers and defenses come free after each Wednesday run.
