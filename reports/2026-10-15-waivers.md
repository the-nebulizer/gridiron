## What changed today

The Friday plan from earlier this week went through overnight: Tyler Bass is added at kicker, Jakobi Meyers is dropped, both for $0 since the pool is still first-come-first-serve. Nothing to do about that now — it's finished business.

## Why this opens a new question

Meyers was the one bench receiver standing behind Stefon Diggs, who's been Questionable with a hamstring issue for two weeks running. With Meyers gone and the other bench receiver, Malik Washington, on his own bye this week, there is currently no legal way to fill the WR slot if Diggs can't play Sunday — confirmed by running the lineup solver with Diggs pulled out: the slot comes back unfillable, not just weaker.

Diggs is still expected to play as of today — nothing in his designation has changed — so there's no claim to make tonight. But if Friday or Sunday's injury report rules him out, don't wait: free agency is open first-come-first-serve right now, so the move is an instant $0 add, not a Wednesday bid.

**The plan, if Diggs is ruled out:** add the best available receiver — Keenan Allen tops the unrostered pool right now, and Jakobi Meyers himself is sitting right back on waivers if you'd rather have the familiar option — and drop Will Reichard to make room. Reichard is the easiest cut: kicker is a streamed position here, not one worth two roster spots, and any kicker on the wire can be picked up fresh for Week 7 when Tyler Bass goes on his own bye. The other bench pieces all have a job this week: Bryce Young is the direct backup if Lamar Jackson's ankle doesn't hold up, and Jared Goff is the only other arm for Week 13, when both Lamar and Cousins are on bye together.

## FAAB and the longer view

FAAB sits at $78. Chicago's Week 10 bye is still the next week nothing on the roster covers at defense — San Francisco, New Orleans, Indianapolis and the Giants are the strongest unrostered options when that week gets close, not a call to make now. Zach Charbonnet's practice window opened last week and his coach has him on an aggressive return timetable; separately, the NFL requires a PUP activation within 21 days of that window opening, which lands around October 22 — worth watching, not acting on, since he isn't confirmed for an actual game yet.
