## Why Charbonnet doesn't change the hunt

He's close to a real NFL role again — a chance to play Sunday against Kansas City after a full season on reserve/PUP for a knee injury — but "close to playing" isn't a tradeable asset. Zero games this season means zero sample to sell on, and the smart move with an unproven, ramping-up handcuff-turned-starter is to let him play himself into value first, not ship him at a discount the week he's cleared to practice. Nothing here opens or closes a trade window; revisit once he's actually suited up a game or two.
