## What changed: Jackson's Out, so put him on IR

Jackson's tag flipped from Questionable to Out overnight, ahead of Sunday's game at Cincinnati. Thursday's and Friday's reports both flagged that the IR slot would start to matter the moment one of Irving or Jackson crossed into that status — Jackson just did. He's never been a drop: he projects as a difference-making starter the moment he's back (lost production over the rest of the season runs to 35 points if he left the roster entirely, more than double any other bench player), so the move is to reserve him, not cut him.

Putting him on IR costs nothing and loses nothing — he's been benched behind Bryce Young all along, so this doesn't touch the lineup. It opens the bench spot that's been full at 5-of-5 since Tuesday's Charbonnet cut, with no QB shortage created: Goff, Young and Cousins remain active behind him, still one more arm than the two the lineup starts in superflex. No week ahead opens a hole from this — it's a pure clean-up move.

That open bench spot is roster room, not a reason to spend — nothing below is good enough to use it tonight.
