## The QB decision, checked against the matchup

Goff projects highest of your three options this week (22.6), with Young next (21.9) and Cousins behind (19.7) — but run those three through who they're actually facing and the order isn't as clean: Green Bay is one of the stingiest matchups against the pass in the league this season, which knocks Goff's opponent-adjusted number down near the bottom of the three, while Cousins's Rams matchup is roughly average and his opponent-adjusted number comes out on top. That's a real conflict between the plain projection and the matchup read, so it got the research rather than a coin flip.

Three things settle it for Goff and Young, not Cousins:

- **Form.** Goff's last three games average 26.9 points in this league's scoring — actually above his full-season average, not a hot streak that's cooling. Young's last three average 22, also strong; he entered this stretch leading the NFL in passing yards. Cousins's last three average 22.3 — solid, but the worst of the three, and his one standout number this week is a matchup grade built on a defense that's only average against the pass, not a plus one.
- **The game environment favors Goff's matchup more than the raw defensive ranking suggests.** Lions–Packers is drawing a total in the high 40s to low 50s with Detroit a slight home favorite — a game expected to have points to go around on both sides, which cuts against treating Green Bay's season-long "stingy" grade as a hard ceiling on Goff this week.
- **Cousins carries real downside the matchup number doesn't see.** He was pulled in the second half of a 45-17 loss to New England three weeks ago for the Raiders' rookie first-round pick, and Las Vegas is a roughly 8-point underdog again this week against the Rams. A bad start and an early hook is a live risk with him in a way it isn't for Goff or Young right now.
