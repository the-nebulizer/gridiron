## Why each change

- **TE — Gesicki in for Kraft.** Kraft's on his bye, so this slot had to move regardless. Between the two bench tight ends, Gesicki is the easy pick: Washington's defense allows 16.9 fantasy points a game to tight ends, 2nd-most in the league, and Gesicki's opponent-adjusted estimate (12.9) nearly doubles Johnson's (6.2 against a tougher Chicago defense). Projection and matchup agree here — not a close call.
- **FLEX — Warren in for Washington.** Both the projection (13.4 vs 7.1) and the matchup-adjusted estimate (13.9 vs 9.1) favor Warren clearly, and the gap is only getting wider: Washington's offense is down to its third different QB of the season (Kyle McCord), with Tua out and Malik Willis also lost to IR. Not a close call either.
- **SUPER_FLEX — Young in for Cousins (keep Goff at QB).** This was the one real decision this week. Raw projections for all three quarterbacks are bunched tight — Young 21.4, Cousins 21.0, Goff 20.1 — which is exactly the kind of gap the matchup has to settle. Once opponent and usage are weighed in, they separate clearly: Goff's adjusted estimate is 22.8, Young's 19.8, Cousins' 17.9. San Francisco (Cousins' opponent, hosting this game in Mexico City) is the stingiest of the three pass defenses — 27th-most generous of 32 to quarterbacks, against Baltimore's middling 16th for Young and Tampa Bay's 22nd for Goff. Cousins looked sharp in his first start back in Minnesota last week (two touchdowns beating Green Bay), but that was against a much softer defense than the one he faces now, and it isn't enough to close a gap San Francisco's defense opens up. With a comfortable projected win this week and the league's median game also in play, there's no reason to chase Cousins' ceiling over the two steadier, better-matched options. Goff and Young both grade ahead of Cousins on the matchup, so Goff keeps the dedicated QB slot and Young takes the flex. One catch worth flagging: there's a standing trade offer out to elembach sending Young for D'Andre Swift, and this league's trade review is same-day, so if elembach accepts before kickoff, Young leaves the roster and Cousins should go back into SUPER_FLEX instead — check Sleeper before setting the lineup.
