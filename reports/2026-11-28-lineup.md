## The roster change, and why it should be undone

Sometime since Friday's report, the live Sleeper lineup swapped tight ends: Mike Gesicki is now starting, and Brock Bowers — who every report this week has had as the clear TE1 — is on the bench. Bowers' tag is unchanged: still Questionable, same ankle issue, same as Friday's report had him. What's new is Friday's practice report, and it's worth seeing plainly: Bowers was limited Wednesday and Thursday, then **didn't practice at all Friday**. Coverage on how much that matters is split — one outlet calls it a bad sign, another says he's still trending toward playing — and the Raiders' coach said the Week 13 bye won't factor into the call either way. Nothing in that report rules him out; it's exactly the kind of news that explains caution, but it isn't a downgrade. The Raiders' own backup plan if Bowers sits is Michael Mayer, which doesn't apply here — Ben's own backup is Gesicki, which is the question this report answers.

On the numbers, it isn't close. Bowers projects 12.0 before any matchup adjustment and 18.3 after — Cleveland allows the 12th-most to tight ends, and over his last three games Bowers is still playing a 68% snap share with 6.7 targets, both healthy, neither trending down. Gesicki projects 6.6 and adjusts to 9.8 against a tougher Saints matchup (6th-most allowed), on a 45% snap share and 3.3 targets. A Questionable tag with a bad-but-not-ruling-out practice report doesn't close an 8.5-point adjusted gap — benching Bowers now, before the Raiders have said anything more than "questionable," gives up expected value for certainty he hasn't actually lost.

**The real decision point is Sunday evening, not right now.** The Raiders-Browns game is Sunday Night Football, which kicks off well after the standard Sunday-morning inactives check and after that day's early games. If Cleveland's final word on Bowers (out Sunday afternoon/evening, per the normal NFL inactive-list timing) rules him out, the right pivot at that point is back to Gesicki — the same move that's live right now, just made on the right information instead of ahead of it. Today's call is to put him back in now, and flag that this slot needs a second look Sunday evening, separate from the morning inactives check, before the Raiders-Browns kickoff.
