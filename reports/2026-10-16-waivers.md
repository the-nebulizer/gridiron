## What changed, and why it doesn't move the needle here

Lamar Jackson's ankle issue is now an official Out for Sunday at Cleveland, confirming what the lineup report already assumed when it benched him for Kirk Cousins earlier this week. A status update on a rostered player doesn't touch the budget or open a bench spot — the bench is still full at 5/5: Malik Washington, Will Reichard, Jared Goff, Lamar Jackson, and Juwan Johnson.

Four quarterbacks is still four quarterbacks — Lamar sitting this week doesn't reduce your QB depth, it just confirms which two of the four start. Being Out does make him IR-eligible under this league's rules, but the one IR slot is already filled by Zach Charbonnet (PUP), so there's no move to make there tonight. If his timeline stretches into multiple weeks and something changes on the IR slot, that's a question for then, not now.
