## What changed, and why it doesn't move the needle here

Kirk Cousins came off the bench into the starting QB slot, and Lamar Jackson — Questionable with an ankle issue that's drawn mixed reports this week — went the other way. That's the lineup report's call to make, not this one's: it's a bench-to-starter swap between two players you already roster, so it doesn't touch the budget, doesn't open a bench spot, and doesn't change anything about who's available on the wire. The bench is still full at 5/5: Malik Washington, Will Reichard, Jared Goff, Juwan Johnson, and now Lamar Jackson in place of Cousins.

Four quarterbacks is still four quarterbacks — Lamar sitting this week doesn't reduce your QB depth, it just rearranges which two of the four start. If his ankle were to turn into something longer (an IR move), that would be a different question; nothing in this week's reporting suggests that yet, and it isn't a call to make in advance of real news.
