## FAAB plan

Neither is urgent enough to spend on today, so the plan is to hold the full $61 and revisit it the moment either Goff or Cousins actually gets hurt, rather than buy insurance against something that hasn't happened.

**No bid on Anthony Richardson (QB IND) — retracting last week's call.** Last Sunday's report recommended $23 to block GehrmannJake from the Colts' new starter. Re-running the numbers tonight, that doesn't hold up anymore: `denial.mjs` shows GehrmannJake would gain +6.9 points rest-of-season and sweeneyb +5 — both under the +8 bar the model uses to call a bubble team's gain a real threat, so there's no supportable block case this week. He'd add zero points to my own lineup (Goff and Cousins are both healthy), so there's no justification left to spend FAAB on him. He's still a legitimate backup-QB-depth want if either of my two QBs gets hurt, but that's a want, not a bid — if he's still on the wire after Wednesday's run, he becomes a free Thursday pickup instead, and I'll take that over paying real money for speculative insurance.

**Drew Lock (QB SEA) and Chris Rodriguez Jr. (RB JAX) — watching, not bidding.** Both are flagged as handcuffs the same way Hill is: Lock backs up Sam Darnold (listed Out for sweeneyb), Rodriguez backs up Bhayshul Tuten (Questionable for sweeneyb).
