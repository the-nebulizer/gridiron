## Revised Tuesday afternoon: keep the Richardson claim

The 7am version of this report retracted the $23 Richardson claim, on the grounds that no rival gains enough from him to justify a block. That was the wrong test. The claim was never really a block; it's **cover**. The outlook lists quarterback as a no-cover position: Goff and Cousins are the only healthy quarterbacks for two quarterback-eligible slots, and Lamar projects 0 through Week 17. "Wait until one gets hurt" doesn't work for three reasons:

- An injury on a Sunday sends every free quarterback to waivers until the next Wednesday, so you'd be bidding blind against the field.
- Richardson is the only free quarterback projected to play at all. Once someone else claims him, no replacement exists.
- FAAB has no value after Week 17.

The card now accepts `cover` as a reason to bid, checked against the outlook's no-cover list, so this claim no longer has to pretend to be a block.

**Justice Hill: no claim.** Richardson takes the one open roster spot, so Hill would need a drop. The points model prices dropping the Broncos at −3.4 over the season and −1.3 in the playoffs. Dropping Juwan Johnson would leave Gesicki alone at tight end while Bowers is Out and on bye in Week 13. The block also isn't worth much: JackRAndrew already carries four running backs (Bijan Robinson, Henry, Jordan Mason, Kyle Monangai), and the model finds no real gain for them from Hill.

**FAAB after the claim:** $61 − $23 = **$38**.
