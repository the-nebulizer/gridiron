## Why Kamara, why now

Last week's check flagged sweeneyb, amandaberry, and sparkJ88 as the only three teams running exactly two healthy quarterbacks with no backup — but concluded there was nothing to offer any of them, because nothing on either side of a deal looked appealing. That changed this week: Travis Etienne (GehrmannJake's RB, not mine or amandaberry's) went to IR with a hamstring injury and won't be back before Week 9. New Orleans' backfield now runs through Kamara, who amandaberry has buried on their bench behind Jahmyr Gibbs and Brian Robinson. He wasn't worth asking for last week; he is now.

My roster is exactly at what it starts at running back (3 active for 3 demanded slots, plus Zach Charbonnet still on IR/PUP) — Jeanty's a rookie, Irving's in a Tampa committee, and Warren byes in Week 9. A game-leading-role back off the wire for the cost of a backup quarterback I don't need is straightforward value.

On the other side: amandaberry starts Trevor Lawrence and Jordan Love and has nobody else at the position — a bye or a bad half from either one empties their SUPER_FLEX into a non-quarterback in a 6-point-passing league, the single worst thing that can happen to a lineup here. Kirk Cousins is the named Raiders starter (beat out rookie Fernando Mendoza for the job in camp), so this isn't a speculative add for them — it's a real fix, for a player who's riding their bench anyway.

Checked the outlook both ways: this trade doesn't fix or open anything on my calendar — Kamara's own Week 8 bye doesn't break my lineup (I still have WR depth to cover the flex), and losing Cousins costs nothing since his bye (Week 13) already overlapped with Lamar's, so he was never my Week 13 answer anyway. Clean swap, no downstream cost.
