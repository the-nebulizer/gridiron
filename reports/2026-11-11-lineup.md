## The Goff question

Your rule is that Goff sits unless his matchup is exceptional. It isn't.

- **The defense is ordinary.** New England allows QBs 19.3 a game, 20th in the league. Green Bay, Cousins's opponent, allows 20.0, 19th. Neither matchup stands out.
- **His form is bad.** Goff's last two games: 12.0 and 6.3. Against Miami he went 17-for-25 for 170 yards, no touchdowns and an interception, and was sacked three times in the first half. He isn't hurt ([NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/player-news/2026-11-08/goff-held-without-a-score-in-week-9-win), [Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/jared-goff-throws-170-yards-212608553.html)). Cousins scored 34.2 and 17.6 in the same two weeks, and his 24.5 season average is the best of your quarterbacks.
- **The game itself is the one point in Goff's favor.** Detroit is a 2.5-point favorite over New England in Munich with a 51.5 total, so the Lions should score about 27. Minnesota is a 3.5-point underdog at Green Bay in a 40.5-total game, about 18.5, and it's Cousins's first game back after Tuesday's trade ([ESPN lines](https://www.espn.com/espn/betting/story/_/id/50414742/2026-nfl-week-10-schedule-odds-point-spreads-totals)).

**What benching him costs:** about 0.4 points on the matchup-adjusted estimates (Goff 21.8, Cousins 21.4) and about 5 points on the raw projections (22.4 vs. 17.1). Those projections were made before the trade and still mark Cousins down for it. This is a close call either way. If it were only my decision, the 27-point implied total would narrowly tip it to Goff. But it's within the noise, and your rule settles it: **Young and Cousins start, Goff sits.**

Young is the safest quarterback this week. Carolina at New Orleans is indoors with a 47.5–48.5 total and roughly even odds. New Orleans allows QBs 21.3 a game (14th), and he projects 22.6, with an opponent-adjusted estimate of 22.5.
