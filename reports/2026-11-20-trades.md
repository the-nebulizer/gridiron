## The rule changed this afternoon

Ben, after the hold: a trade that costs points this week shouldn't be refused if it's clearly better overall. Leave a note instead. The card now does exactly that. A trade has to be ahead over the rest of the season on both projections and matchups, and not worse in any playoff week (single elimination). A loss this week shows up as a note next to the move instead of cancelling it.

Under that rule I re-scanned every QB, RB, WR and TE on the other 11 rosters against 12 packages I could give. 143 deals qualify for me. This is the one that's also good for the other team.

The Warren version is only modestly ahead when JSN is healthy and well behind if he isn't. It also leaves Irving and a slumping Jeanty as the only backs. It isn't certain, so it's off the card.
