## Revised Friday: playoffs are single elimination, so each playoff week is judged on its own

Ben's point: one loss in the playoffs ends the season. Summing the three playoff weeks hides the one that matters most. A trade that loses points in the first playoff game, and gains them in the two games after, only pays off if you survive the first one.

- **The format.** Seven teams make it, Weeks 15–17. In Sleeper's seven-team bracket only the top seed skips Week 15. You're 13–7, tied with JackRAndrew (same division, 0.44 points ahead on points scored) and Tally241. The bye is reachable, but not locked.
- **Lamar won't reliably cover Week 15.** He had thumb surgery with a 6–10 week timeline. ESPN reports "a chance" he's back for Week 16; some reports point later ([ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50472222/ravens-lamar-jackson-injury-typical-line-6-10-weeks), [Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/article/ravens-qb-lamar-jackson-undergoes-thumb-surgery-expected-to-be-out-6-10-weeks-151001861.html)).
- **Young for St. Brown, week by week on matchups:** W15 −7.5, W16 +6.1, W17 +7.2. Week 15 is the game where Goff faces Minnesota, the hardest quarterback matchup in the league, with Young gone. On projections Week 15 is +2.6, but the matchup is real and it's the week you can't lose. **Not certain, so withdraw it.**
- **Young for Swift:** W15 −5.8 on matchups, the same problem. **Withdraw it.**
- **Young for Jaxon Smith-Njigba** is the only deal ahead in every playoff week on both methods: W15 +5.1 to +8.4, W16 +8.6 to +10.7, W17 +10.0 to +11.2, and +58 to +64 over the rest of the season. Its one cost is this week: JSN is on bye, so you lose 1–2 points in Week 11. The card's rule refuses any trade that isn't better this week too, so it isn't on the card. It's also a long shot: JSN is GehrmannJake's best player, and the deal costs them 18 for the season on matchups. Sending it is Ben's call.
- **Holding keeps Young** as the Week 15 quarterback if you end up playing that week, and as cover for Goff or Cousins until Lamar is back.
