# Week 1 trade hunt — 2026-09-14

**Verdict: two ideas worth sending.** Both use the same asset — your extra depth at quarterback — to fix the one real hole on your roster: running back. Read off a fresh sync taken Monday morning after the Week 1 games; nothing here is more than a day old.

## The situation

You're carrying four quarterbacks (Lamar, Goff, Cousins, Young) in a league where everyone else has two or three. That's real currency in this scoring — six-point passing touchdowns in a starter-required superflex slot make even a fringe starting QB worth more than the generic trade charts say. But it's more than you need: Cousins doesn't even cover your Week 13 problem (he's on bye that week too, same as Lamar), and Young is a low-upside stash. Meanwhile your running back room is thin — Jeanty, Irving and Warren are your only healthy backs, with Charbonnet stuck on the injured list. One bad Sunday at RB and you're starting a waiver-wire body.

Two other teams are in the mirror-image spot: plenty of running backs, no backup quarterback at all.

## Idea 1 (best shot): Kirk Cousins + Jakobi Meyers → Tally241, for Aaron Jones

**Tally241's roster:** starts Drake Maye and Bo Nix at quarterback and carries zero backup — if either gets hurt or hits a bye, they're stuck. They're deep at running back (four backs rostered) and Aaron Jones is buried on their bench even though he's playing a real role in Minnesota's backfield this year, splitting passing-down work with Jordan Mason rather than sitting as a pure handcuff.

**Why they plausibly say yes:** Cousins was just named the Raiders' actual Week 1 starter — this isn't a camp arm, it's a locked-in starting QB dropping into a roster that currently has nobody behind Maye or Nix. Meyers sweetens it without costing you anything that matters (he's buried behind Diggs, McMillan and Allen on your own bench). In return you get a running back with a real weekly role, not just an injury-only lottery ticket.

**Send in the app:**
> Hey — I've got more QB depth than I need and saw you're thin behind Maye/Nix. Would you do Kirk Cousins + Jakobi Meyers for Aaron Jones? Cousins is Vegas's actual Week 1 starter, so it's a real QB2/bye-week option for you, not a stash. I could use another back with an actual role given how thin my RB room is right now.

## Idea 2 (fallback, cheaper): Bryce Young → amandaberry, for Brian Robinson Jr.

**amandaberry's roster:** starts Trevor Lawrence and Jordan Love with no backup quarterback at all — bigger hole than Tally241's, since they don't even have a fringe arm. They're carrying five running backs, and Brian Robinson is pure handcuff insurance behind Bijan Robinson right now — real value only if Bijan gets hurt, so he costs them little to move.

**Why they plausibly say yes:** Young is a low-cost body for a team with truly nothing behind their two starters — better than nothing, and it doesn't ask them to give up a back they're actually playing. For you, it's a second, cheaper shot at the same fix (more RB bodies) without touching Cousins.

**Send in the app:**
> You're carrying zero backup QB behind Lawrence/Love — I've got extra arms. Would you take Bryce Young for Brian Robinson? He's a real handcuff play for you and I could use another running back given how thin my bench is there right now.

## If both fall through

Hold the quarterback depth rather than give it away for less than this. It's still tradeable later — once another team loses a QB to injury, or as Week 13 approaches (Lamar and Cousins share that bye), you'll have leverage again. Standing pat costs nothing.

Games have only been played once, so treat both as opening offers, not final ones — expect a counter and don't be afraid to hold if the other side lowballs you. It's early (Week 1, everyone's 0-0), so no urgency here; the honest contender read comes at Week 9, ahead of the Week 11 trade deadline.
