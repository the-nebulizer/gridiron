## Why this morning's offer won't land

This morning's report offered Juwan Johnson for Swift. The idea was that Swift was "buried on their bench" and that elembach needed a tight end. Both points were wrong.

- **Swift isn't buried.** He was on elembach's bench last week only because Chicago had its bye. He's their best back by a distance: he projects 15.7 a game for the rest of the season, with Rhamondre Stevenson at 9.5 and Zach Charbonnet at 8.6. This week Charbonnet and Stevenson are on bye, so Swift is their only healthy starting back.
- **They don't need a tight end.** Johnson (7.6 a game) would back up Brenton Strange (7.2), not replace him.

Run through the same points model on elembach's own roster, the Johnson deal costs them 10.5 points this week and 55 over the rest of the season. No manager accepts that. The case for sending it also assumed nothing was lost, which is false here: an obvious lowball can sour the one conversation that still matters before the deadline.

## What elembach actually needs: a quarterback this week

- **Geno Smith** left Week 10 with an ankle injury. He has no timeline, and Sleeper lists him as Out ([NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/player-news/2026-11-15/geno-smith-heads-to-locker-room-with-ankle-injury), [Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/geno-smith-suffers-ankle-injury-203513314.html)).
- **Michael Penix Jr.** is on bye in Week 11.
- **That leaves Josh Allen alone at quarterback**, with $0 of FAAB. Their best option is a free backup after Wednesday's run.

Bryce Young fills that hole. He projects 22.0 this week, and he's averaged 21.4 this season in this scoring.

## What it does for me

The trade projects better in every window: **+6.5 this week, +17.7 over the next three, +29.3 for the rest of the season, +8.4 in the playoffs.** Swift takes Jeanty's RB slot (17.8 projected this week to Jeanty's 10.4). Cousins moves into SUPER_FLEX, a 1.8-point drop from Young this week. It opens no empty slot in any week through the playoffs.

**The cost, plainly:** quarterback depth drops to Goff and Cousins. Lamar Jackson is the third only if he's back from surgery, which can't be before Week 14. If Goff or Cousins misses a game before then, SUPER_FLEX goes to a waiver backup (the $45 reserve covers that). Even so, the gain is large and the exposure is one game slot at worst. On Ben's certain-or-nothing standard it clears. Swift's own risk is the one this morning's report named: Chicago has talked about splitting carries, and Kyle Monangai (toe, Out) will return at some point. Swift has averaged 15.8 for the season and 15.7 over his last three even so.

## How likely elembach is to accept

**Maybe one in four.** For them it's +4.1 this week and about −34 over the rest of the season, assuming Geno is back next week. The longer Geno's ankle keeps him out, the more Young is worth to them. A manager on the playoff bubble with no FAAB and an empty SUPER_FLEX has a real reason to say yes. The model doesn't show a better offer: every other package I have to give (Jeanty, Washington, Johnson added on) costs them 30 or more for the season. Cousins or Goff in Young's place would make it worse for me in the playoffs, so neither clears the bar.

If they decline, nothing is lost. Jeanty keeps the RB slot and Young stays as quarterback cover. This is the last trade week.
