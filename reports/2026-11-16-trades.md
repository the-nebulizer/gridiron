## The mismatch: elembach has zero TE depth, and a buried RB worth more than he's being used for

A fresh scan of all 12 rosters (`data/league/snapshot.json`) turns up one clean trade: three teams — elembach, Tally241, and sparkJ88 — carry exactly one tight end with nobody behind him. Of the three, elembach is the one holding a bench piece worth more than the ask: D'Andre Swift, sitting behind Zach Charbonnet and Rhamondre Stevenson (who start) and Omarion Hampton (who doesn't).

Swift's usage has turned into a real lead role — 78.3% of snaps and 20.3 carries a game over his last three, with 3.7 red-zone touches, well up from his 64.6%/17.2 season averages. Sleeper's own projections have him at 14-18 points a week the rest of the way (`node scripts/value.mjs --add 6790`). The reason shows up elsewhere on this same snapshot: Kyle Monangai, Swift's main competition in Chicago's backfield, is rostered on JackRAndrew's bench and tagged **Out** right now — exactly the kind of role claim the prime directive says can't be taken on memory, so it's worth having it confirmed live in-league rather than just in the news.

Meanwhile my own Ashton Jeanty has gone the other way. Recent reporting has his carries sliding 23→21→19→15→10→10→8→2 across the season, bottoming out at a career-low 2 carries in his most recent game as the Raiders hand the job to Mike Washington; the coordinator has called it a week-to-week split for now, not a reversal.

Juwan Johnson is pure zero to my own lineup — `scripts/value.mjs` shows losing him costs 0 points this week, rest of season, and in the playoffs, because he never plays behind Tucker Kraft. He's still a real streaming-grade TE on the open market (8.1 avg), which is exactly what elembach needs and doesn't have. Mike Gesicki stays on my bench either way, so Kraft's Week 11 bye is still covered regardless of how this trade goes.

**The trade math** (`node scripts/value.mjs --trade --give 7002 --get 6790`): even for a week where Swift is on CHI's bye and contributes nothing yet, this projects +12.2 over the next three weeks, +32.3 rest of season, and +10.3 in the playoffs — no week gets worse. `node scripts/outlook.mjs --add 6790 --drop 7002` confirms it opens no holes and still covers every remaining bye.

**The real risk, stated plainly:** Chicago's staff has said more than once this season that it wants to spread the backfield load, and that preference predates Monangai's current injury — if Monangai (or another back) comes back healthy, Swift's workload could shrink again. That's a role risk, not a certainty either way. It doesn't flip this trade, though: the entire cost on my side is a player who is worth exactly nothing to my own lineup right now. Even a Swift who reverts to a timeshare still very likely outproduces a Jeanty who just got benched down to 2 carries, and the downside if Swift's role shrinks back is that I've traded a $0 asset for a bench back — not a loss either way.

**Why elembach plausibly says yes:** I'm not asking them to bench a starter — Swift is already on their bench this week (his own bye), and Hampton, not Swift, is their other committee back behind Charbonnet/Stevenson. In return they go from zero TE depth to a legitimate streaming option behind Strange, heading into the stretch where a missed bye or ding at TE would otherwise cost them a scoring slot outright.
