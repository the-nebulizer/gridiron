## The one close call left: Goff/Young over Cousins at QB

The matchup sheet's opponent-adjusted numbers flip the order at quarterback: Jared Goff (22.6 projected) faces the stingiest pass defense of the three — Green Bay allows the 28th-fewest points to QBs — for an adjusted estimate of 19. Bryce Young (21.7 projected) faces a middling Tampa Bay defense for an adjusted 20.5. Kirk Cousins, on the bench, projects lowest of the three at 19.3 against a similarly middling Rams defense, but grades out highest once adjusted, at 23.8.

Researched it rather than trusting either number alone: Las Vegas is a home underdog in a high-total game Sunday (Rams favored by 4.5–5.5, total near 47–48.5), the kind of script that usually means more passing volume for the trailing team — a real reason Cousins' ceiling could be live. But it cuts the other way too: Cousins' own production doesn't back it up yet. His season average (24.0/g) and last-three-game average (22.3) both trail Goff's (27.8, 26.9) and sit about even with Young's (26.9, 22.0), and the sample behind that 23.8 adjusted number is thin enough that one plus-matchup week shouldn't override two healthy starters who are outproducing him. Baker Mayfield's questionable status for Tampa Bay (rookie Jalon Daniels could start instead) is a mild complication for Young's own matchup read, but not enough on its own to swap in a third arm. Holding Goff and Young — the floor play, which matters doubly this week since the league also scores against the median. Worth re-checking if Cousins' role or production shifts, but not tonight.

Jordan Love's participation for Green Bay is worth a last Saturday-night check before locking in — if backup Tyrod Taylor plays instead, the Packers' own passing game typically gets worse across the board, which would cut into Tucker Kraft's target share. Kraft's matchup is strong enough to hold through that either way (Detroit allows the most fantasy points to tight ends of any defense this season), but it's the one thing left to confirm before Sunday.
