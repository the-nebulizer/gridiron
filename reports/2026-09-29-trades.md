## The offer: Cousins for Tony Pollard

Read off a fresh sync this morning, with Week 4 ahead. SchoaTime (2-4) has two quarterbacks, and Baker Mayfield dislocated his thumb on Sunday. Tampa says he'll miss [at least three weeks](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/todd-bowles-baker-mayfield-out-a-minimum-of-three-weeks-with-thumb-dislocation). That leaves Brock Purdy alone, with a hole in their SUPER_FLEX through roughly Week 6. In a league with 6-point passing TDs, that empty slot costs them more every week than anything else on their roster.

**Why Cousins and not Bryce Young:** Young's bye is Week 5, in the middle of Baker's absence, so he'd cover only two of the three weeks. Cousins doesn't have a bye until 13 and covers all of them. He's also the easier one for me to lose. With Cousins gone I still have Lamar, Goff and Young. Young covers Goff's Week 6 bye and Lamar's Week 13 bye, so the outlook shows the trade opens no holes anywhere on the calendar.

**Why Pollard:** He's Tennessee's lead back. In Week 3 he had [17 carries and all 4 of his targets](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/notes/tony-pollard.php) while Spears dealt with his ankle. That's a low-end RB2 who's sitting on SchoaTime's bench behind Chase Brown and Jadarian Price. For me he's depth, not a starter. Jeanty, Irving and Warren stay in the lineup, and Pollard becomes the RB4 my running back room doesn't have right now (Charbonnet is still stuck on PUP). He's also the better back than either one I've been chasing: RJ Harvey is Questionable and splits snaps in a committee, and Brian Robinson backs up Bijan. One thing to know: Pollard and Warren share a Week 9 bye. The outlook still fields a full lineup that week.

**Don't ask for more.** Cam Ward and Jalon Daniels (Mayfield's own replacement) are both sitting on waivers, so SchoaTime can fill the slot for free. That caps my leverage. Brian Thomas Jr. is the player on their bench I'd really want, but asking for him gets a "no thanks" and a waiver pickup. A bench back for a quarterback who starts every week is a deal they can accept.

## What happens to the offers already out

- **Cousins for Harvey (ObiWanless):** leave it open. Both offers give Cousins, and Sleeper cancels the other one when the first is accepted, so you can't end up trading him twice. Pollard is the one I'd rather land. If ObiWanless accepts first, that's still a decent result.
- **Bryce Young for Brian Robinson (amandaberry):** withdraw it. It was only ever a fallback, and Pollard is the better version of the same move. If it were accepted after Cousins leaves, I'd be down to Lamar and Goff with nobody for their byes.
- **Ollie Gordon waiver claim:** unchanged. It still only runs if the Harvey deal doesn't land. If the Pollard trade processes before Wednesday, I have a fourth back and would cancel the Gordon claim. It was a speculative stash anyway.

Seven weeks to the Week 11 deadline. Kicker in Week 7 and defense in Week 8 are the real holes on the calendar, and both are waiver fixes, not trade fixes. Every playoff week is fully fillable today.
