> Refreshed Oct 16 after a roster change: Lamar Jackson now Out

## What changed, and why it doesn't open anything

Lamar Jackson's ankle issue is now a confirmed Out for Sunday, settling what was a game-time question when yesterday's hunt ran — but settling a one-week question isn't a change in what he's worth. He's still your best-performing healthy quarterback this season (23 points a game), and the rest-of-season model still favors him well ahead of Cousins and Young once his ankle clears. A single-week designation is a reason to sit him, not a reason to sell low on him.

## Rechecked against the full league

Today's activity log shows only routine waiver churn across the league — a kicker swap here, a backup quarterback add there, nothing that signals a rival losing a starter or suddenly needing what you have surplus of. The standing read from yesterday's full 12-roster hunt still holds:

- Kicker and defense are the only real thin spots here, and nobody trades those mid-season — that's a weekly streaming question, not a trade one.
- Four quarterbacks for two superflex starting spots is real depth, but every quarterback-needy team in the league is already starting both of its own, so there's still no motivated buyer.
- No other roster is sitting on a running back or receiver that would clearly upgrade a spot in this lineup.

## Standing watch

The trade deadline is Week 11 — five weeks out, no change. Nothing on the forward outlook shows a playoff-week hole that can't still be fixed on waivers before then. The one thing worth watching, not acting on: Lamar being Out does make him IR-eligible, but the one IR slot is already filled by Zach Charbonnet (PUP), so that's not a lever tonight either. If his timeline stretches into multiple weeks, his trade value — and the case for moving a spare quarterback instead — could look different by Monday's hunt; that's a question for fresh news, not for tonight.
