## Charbonnet was never a tradeable asset, and now he's gone

He'd been built up in the last couple of reports as a running back possibly worth holding for value once he played himself into a role. Cutting him before he logged a single snap this season closes that out — zero games played means there was never a sample to sell on, and now there's nothing to weigh. No loss to the hunt either way; it just removes a name that was going to keep coming up as a maybe.

Buying a small point edge now while opening a real hole right as the deadline closes isn't the trade to make. Pass, same conclusion as the last two hunts.
