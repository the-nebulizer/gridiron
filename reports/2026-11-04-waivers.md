## The tight end

Mike Gesicki averages 9.8 a game and has played half the snaps for Cincinnati all year, up to 68% last week. He plays Atlanta this week and Washington in Week 11, which is the week Kraft is on bye and the Washington defense gives up the second-most to tight ends. With Lamar on IR you have a free bench spot, so no one has to go.

## The defense

Houston is the best defense matchup of the next two weeks (the Chargers this week, Cleveland in Week 10 when Denver is on bye). It is not an action on the card: free agency reopens after Wednesday's run, so the card would normally have you wait and add Houston for $0. Houston is also trending hard league-wide, so a $1 claim now with Cleveland as the drop is a reasonable way to avoid losing them. The Chargers line is about a pick'em (Chargers by 2.5, total 43.5); Houston's safeties Blankenship and Pitre are both questionable.
