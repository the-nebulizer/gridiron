# League activity — through Thursday, Sep 10

**Two things worth acting on: Michael Mayer is unrostered with Brock Bowers likely out, and Khalil Shakir has been sitting unclaimed for a week.**

Nine moves have been made in this league since the draft. Six of the players let go were never picked up by anyone, and two of them are better than the worst piece on your bench.

## Claim-worthy right now

**Michael Mayer (TE, LV)** — the most important available player on the board, and the one to claim first. Brock Bowers is Doubtful with a meniscus tear, so Mayer inherits the Raiders' starting tight end job, and the market has noticed: about 1.7 million managers have added him, more than anyone else on Sleeper this week. Nobody in this league has him. You carry exactly one tight end, so if Kraft misses a week you have nothing — Mayer fixes that and is a startable piece on his own while Bowers is out. Malik Davis is the drop.

**Khalil Shakir (WR, BUF)** — elembach dropped him on Sep 3 to take Alec Pierce for $1. He's one of Josh Allen's primary targets, he's been unclaimed in a 12-team superflex league for a week, and the wider market is net-positive on him (about 72,000 managers adding against 55,000 dropping). Malik Davis is the weakest thing you're holding — no standalone role in Dallas — but if Mayer takes that spot, Keenan Allen is the next drop.

**Devaughn Vele (WR, NO)** — still unrostered, and among the most-added players on the board: roughly 327,000 adding against 53,000 dropping, with Jordyn Tyson out about two months. Tuesday's report called for him as a free instant add; that window closed at kickoff, so he's a Wednesday claim now.

Three claims need three drops, and you only have two you'd want to make (Davis, Allen). Mayer first — he covers a hole nothing else on your bench covers. Between the two receivers, Vele has the stronger market behind him; Shakir has the better quarterback throwing to him.
