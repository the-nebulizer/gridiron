## What changed today

Stefon Diggs and Lamar Jackson both moved from Out to Questionable. That's good news, and it actually strengthens tonight's plan rather than changing it: Diggs now looks like your better starting receiver this week (see today's lineup report), which makes Jakobi Meyers — not Diggs — the one with the least to lose from your roster. Nothing about the Friday kicker plan changes.

## What to do

1. **Tonight: nothing.** No claim, no bid, no drop.
2. **Wednesday morning** waivers process, and from then until kickoff any unclaimed player can be added instantly for $0 — that's held true every week this season.
3. **Friday, after final injury designations:** add **Nick Folk** (Atlanta, home vs Chicago, projects 8.5) and **drop Jakobi Meyers**. If Folk is gone, take Cairo Santos or Tyler Bass.
4. Lineup moves for Sunday are covered in today's lineup report, not here.

## Why Meyers is still the drop

Your real surplus is the receiver group behind Tetairoa McMillan: Meyers, Diggs and Washington all project in the same range, and only one or two of them start in any given week. With Diggs trending healthier and valued as a season-long piece, Meyers is the one with the smallest gap to the alternative and the one the numbers have supported as the cheapest cut for a week now. Young and Lamar stay — Young is your highest-scoring quarterback this year and your only clean cover if Lamar's ankle doesn't hold up; dropping either would cost real points this season.

## Kicker and defense, further out

Chicago's Week 10 bye is still the next week nothing on your roster can cover at defense — Indianapolis, San Francisco, New Orleans and the Giants are the best unrostered options when that week gets close, not a call to make now. Charbonnet is still tracking to be activated from the PUP spot around Week 7; when that happens, the cheapest spot to free will again come from the Meyers/Diggs/Washington group.

FAAB stays at $78.
