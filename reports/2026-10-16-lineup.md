## The one thing to watch going forward

With the designation official, the open question is no longer this week's lineup — it's how long the ankle keeps him out. Nothing to do about that tonight: the IR slot is full (Zach Charbonnet, PUP), so even though "Out" now makes Lamar IR-eligible under this league's rules, there's no open slot to move him into, and doing so wouldn't free anything this bench isn't already carrying. If Charbonnet comes off PUP or the timeline stretches into multiple weeks, that's a real decision — not one today's information supports yet.

This season his healthy average (23/game) is still the best of any quarterback on this roster, and the rest-of-season model rates him far ahead of both Cousins and Young once he's back. This is a bench, not a value judgment — nothing here calls for a drop or a trade.
