## The one claim that clears the bar

De'Von Achane tore his ACL against Kansas City and is done for the year. That's the single biggest running-back story in the league right now, and Ollie Gordon — Miami's backup — is sitting unrostered in our league while he's been scooped up almost everywhere else. It's not a clean bell-cow situation yet: Jaylen Wright is actually listed ahead of Gordon on Miami's own depth chart, but Wright himself was out Week 3 with a foot injury and it's not clear he's back for Thursday's slate. Until we see how Miami actually splits the touches, Gordon goes on the bench behind Jaylen Warren rather than into the lineup — Warren just posted a 90%-snap workhorse game and is favored to do it again this week with Rico Dowdle still not practicing.

The drop is Jakobi Meyers — checked against the season ahead, dropping him opens no new holes (dropping either Keenan Allen or Mike Gesicki instead would; Gesicki is the only Week 11 tight end cover once Tucker Kraft's bye hits, and Allen is needed to cover Week 7 when Diggs and Meyers are both out). Meyers is the one bench spot with no forward cost attached.

**One thing to flag rather than bury:** this claim is written `if_not` today's pending Kirk Cousins-for-RJ Harvey trade (and, one level down, the Bryce Young-for-Brian Robinson fallback). Both of those also bring home a depth running back, and running back isn't a need right now — we start 3 and carry exactly 3 active. Landing Gordon on top of either trade would put 5 backs on a roster with 3 starting spots, which is exactly the kind of unlinked pile-up that's bitten this league before. The trade offers were made first and are further along (an accepted offer with 0-day review processes before Wednesday's waivers even run), so they get the roster spot if either lands. If both trades fall through, take Gordon — of the three, he's the one with a real shot at a season-long starting job, not just a change-of-pace role.

$9 is the ceiling here, not a lowball — with Gordon not (yet) projected into the lineup and running back not a thin position, the going rate for pure speculation is capped at 10% of the $92 remaining. If Wright is out again and Gordon looks like the clear early-down back after Sunday, next week's report is where he'd get priced as a real add and matched up against Warren directly.
