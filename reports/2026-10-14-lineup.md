## What changed today

Two of your bench players just got better news: Stefon Diggs and Lamar Jackson both moved off the Out list and onto Questionable. Neither is a lock yet — more on that below — but it reopens two of the three dead lineup spots this week with real starters instead of fallback bodies.

## The three dead spots nobody fixed yet

Three of your ten starters are on bye this week and currently sitting in your lineup anyway: Jared Goff at quarterback, Malik Washington at receiver, and Will Reichard at kicker. All three would score zero on Sunday if left in place. Two of them have better options sitting right on your bench:

- **Quarterback:** Lamar Jackson projects 22.9 points if he plays — better than anyone else on your roster — and his ankle has improved from Out to Questionable. That's a real but not final step: his coach wasn't yet sure Wednesday whether he'd even practice. If Thursday or Friday's report says he's out again, Bryce Young is the clean fallback — he's your highest-scoring quarterback this season and was already being held in reserve for exactly this.
- **Receiver:** Tetairoa McMillan is the automatic call here — no injury question, he simply projects better (11.2) than a bye-week zero.
- **Kicker:** nobody on your bench plays kicker. This stays empty until Friday's waiver pickup — see this week's waiver report.

## One more swap, no injury involved

Tucker Kraft projects a half-point better than Juwan Johnson at tight end this week on a more favorable matchup — not an injury call, just the better number. Worth making even though nothing forces it.

## The receiver call that's closer

Stefon Diggs also looks like the right starting receiver over Jakobi Meyers this week — he projects a point and a half higher now that he's no longer Out. But his hamstring status for Sunday isn't locked in as of Wednesday; if the week's injury reports turn worse, Meyers slides back in. Sunday's inactives check will make the final call either way.

## What's still ahead

Nothing here touches the bigger picture: Chicago's Week 10 bye is still the next week your defense can't be covered, and that's a waiver question closer to the date, not a lineup one today.
