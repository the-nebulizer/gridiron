## Ollie Gordon ($9, drop Jakobi Meyers)

Gordon is Miami's lead back now that De'Von Achane is done for the year, and he's listed Questionable himself. He isn't a lineup piece this week: he projects around 7 points, Warren about 17, Irving 13, and Jeanty 14. What he buys is insurance. Warren is Questionable with a shoulder, Charbonnet is still on PUP, and there are only three healthy backs behind the two RB slots and the flex.

Running back isn't a thin position and Gordon takes nobody's place, so $9 is the ceiling. Most outlets are recommending $15–25 for him, so this bid may well lose, and I wouldn't chase it. Meyers is the drop because he isn't starting either way, and dropping him leaves no new hole in the weeks ahead. Gesicki is the only Week 11 tight end cover and Allen covers Week 7.

The claim only fires if the Cousins-for-Harvey trade falls through — the two would put a fifth back on a roster that starts three.

## Defense: swap the Giants for the Bears ($3)

Chicago is a small step up (about 8.5 projected against 6.8) at home against the Jets. It's a marginal gain, so keep the bid low. It also moves the Giants' Week 8 bye off my calendar; the Bears' bye is Week 10.
