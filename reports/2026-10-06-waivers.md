## Why the last call doesn't stand

The previous check priced a $5 add of Keon Coleman against dropping Jakobi Meyers, on the read that Meyers was a buried bench receiver. Since then, Tetairoa McMillan's Week 5 bye pulled him out of the starting lineup and Meyers moved into that receiver spot next to Stefon Diggs. He's a starter now, not a cut — so that move is off the board.

Checked every other bench spot for a clean drop before giving up on Coleman entirely:

- **Mike Gesicki (TE)** — dropping him reopens the Week 11 tight end hole; he's the only cover for Tucker Kraft's bye that week.
- **Keenan Allen (WR, Out)** — dropping him opens a real Week 7 hole: Diggs, Meyers, and Coleman himself all bye that week, and Allen is the only other healthy receiver on the roster to cover it.
- **Bryce Young (QB)** — the cleanest of the three, but still a real cost: it leaves only Goff and Jackson at quarterback for Week 13, when Cousins and Jackson both bye, downgrading SUPER_FLEX to a non-quarterback that week.

Every bench spot is protecting something specific on the calendar. Coleman himself is a nice but non-urgent speculative piece — Buffalo's receiving corps thinned out when Khalil Shakir went down — and he's worth a second look if a roster spot opens up on its own (an injury, a bye-week cut) rather than forcing one open this week.
