## Why the Kamara offer is off

Your rule from now on: an offer goes out only when it's certain you're better off making it than not. Yesterday's Cousins-for-Kamara idea doesn't meet that bar:

- **Kamara wouldn't start for you.** Your three backs fill both running back spots and the flex, so he'd be bench depth, not a lineup upgrade.
- **He's banged up.** He has played through knee and back knocks the last two weeks, and the case for him rested on that holding.
- **It costs quarterback depth when you're short on it.** Goff is on bye this week and Lamar's status for Sunday is unknown. In a superflex league with 6-point passing touchdowns, Cousins is worth more to you than a backup running back.

No other offer is on the table this week. Monday's trade hunt will look across the league again under the new rule. The trade deadline is Week 11, five weeks away.
